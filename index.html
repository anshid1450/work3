About GENUS

Genus Innovative Solutions

Genus, a subsidiary of Ali Mohammad Abdulla General Trading LLC (AMAGT), based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. We are a leading provider of innovative industrial solutions. With a decade of excellence, we specialize in delivering premium standard equipment and strategic supply chain management for the industrial sector.
